Important reminders

  • Safety comes first.
  • Report every accident or contact incident involving company vehicles or equipment, regardless of how minor it appears.
  • Never speculate, alter records, or provide false information.
  • Do not post accident details or photos on social media.
  • Do not discuss the incident with the media or make commitments on behalf of UNIVERSAL TRAFFIC.
  • Follow company policy and applicable reporting requirements.
Always secure the scene if safe to do so, and contact the D&H office as soon as safely possible.
